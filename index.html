🧭 Outcome Areas, Leads and Targets
🌾 Food Security
Strengthened food systems and agriculture productivity.
→ Click to explore details
Lead: WFP, FAO
  • Support 2.5–3 million people with integrated food security assistance in IPC Phase 4+ areas.
  • Reduce IPC Phase 3+ by 10%, equivalent to around 770,000 people moving to IPC Phase 2 or better.
  • Provide climate-smart agriculture inputs to 100,000 smallholder farmers.
  • Expand climate-resilient cultivation across 10,000 hectares.
  • Contribute 20,000–30,000 metric tons of locally produced food.
🏥 Basic / Essential Social Services
Sustained access to health, education, skills development and nutrition services.
→ Click to explore details
Lead: WHO, UNICEF, NGO Forum
  • 3.2 million people in need access essential health services, including HIV, TB and Malaria services.
  • 200,000 out-of-school children and adolescents enrolled in formal or non-formal education.
  • 150,000 crisis-affected children supported with temporary learning spaces, materials and psychosocial support.
  • 600,000 lower primary children reached with foundational literacy and numeracy interventions.
  • 1.2 million children with SAM/MAM receive treatment.
  • 816 health facilities deliver the full Essential Health Services Package.
🏘 Durable Solutions
Reduced number of people in prolonged displacement, including refugees.
→ Click to explore details
Lead: UNDP, IOM, UNHCR, NGO Forum
  • 60,000 displaced persons receive targeted assistance for sustainable return, local integration or relocation.
  • Closure of 1 camp in Wau.
  • 75% of displacement-affected people in potential solutions areas have access to basic services.
  • 5 government-led area-based durable solutions roadmaps or action plans developed or updated.
  • At least 3 state-level durable solutions coordination mechanisms operationalized.
⚡ Rapid Response
Life-saving assistance and protection following new shocks.
→ Click to explore details
Lead: UNHCR, NRC
  • 65% of people in need following a new shock reached with first-line life-saving protection and assistance.
  • 40% of annual new shock-affected caseload reached within 14 days.
  • Protection mainstreamed and embedded in 100% of response.
  • Early warning and disaster preparedness mechanisms strengthened.
🕊 Peace, Governance and Justice
Community violence reduction, civic engagement, rule of law, access to justice and human rights.
→ Click to explore details
Lead: UNDP, UNMISS
  • At least 12 community peace committees or platforms operational and engaging in conflict mitigation.
  • 30,000 people reached with civic education on constitution-making, elections and transitional justice.
  • Mobile access to justice deployed in at least 8 conflict-affected locations.
  • 300 justice sector actors have enhanced skills and capacities.
  • 3,000–4,000 individuals receive legal aid through mobile clinics and paralegal partners.
  • 600 duty bearers have enhanced SGBV response capacity in 5–6 counties.
